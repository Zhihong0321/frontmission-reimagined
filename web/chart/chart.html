What you are looking at

Real data: the 20 cities, 29 roads, terrain multipliers, biome regions, projection and the Mule truck's speed and fuel come from the game's data/ folder.
Illustrative: Host claims and nests, ruins, dead villages, rail yards, refineries, solar farms, radio masts, woods and copses, pylons, wrecks, smoke and the grey are a visual proposal, not game content yet.
The convoy's shape is real: the sprite is picked to match the truck class you are driving.
Driving: WASD drives the convoy anywhere. Off-road speed follows the biome; mountains and water block unless you are on a road. Click any point to pathfind there. Roll into a city to park.
Time: one day passes every 2.4 s at 1× while moving.
Optional art: drop textures into art/ (see README) and they are used automatically.
